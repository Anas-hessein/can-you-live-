Ink and Blood Effects
=====================

Combat effects: blood hit, slash, drops in several sizes and a pool that grows, ink
splash and the ink seal that closes a defeated enemy. Blood is the only red.

Format: PNG with transparency (RGBA), unless noted. Pivots are in pixels from the
top-left corner of each frame or image. Sprite sheets are a single row of frames
of the listed size, left to right.

Animations
----------
blood_hit: 3 frames, 512x384 px each, 12 fps, once, pivot (256, 192)
blood_slash: 3 frames, 512x384 px each, 12 fps, once, pivot (256, 192)
blood_drop_sizes: 3 frames, 512x384 px each, 6 fps, once, pivot (256, 340)
blood_pool: 3 frames, 512x384 px each, 6 fps, once, pivot (256, 340)
ink_splash: 3 frames, 512x384 px each, 12 fps, once, pivot (256, 340)
ink_seal: 5 frames, 512x640 px each, 8 fps, once, pivot (256, 320)

Hitodama flames
---------------
Five white will-o'-the-wisp flames with soft edges; the flame's brightness is its alpha, so
draw them with additive or screen blending over dark backgrounds. Not an animation: pick one
per spirit, or cycle them slowly.

Shrine Props and Decoration
===========================

Breakable props (jizo, paper lantern, shoji door, each intact and broken), shrine
decoration, foreground hair, drips and bamboo, ink trees, paper tears, veins, pickups,
a stone lantern checkpoint and a ground eye.

Format: PNG with transparency (RGBA), unless noted. Pivots are in pixels from the
top-left corner of each frame or image. Sprite sheets are a single row of frames
of the listed size, left to right.

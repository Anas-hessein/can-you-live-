Sumi, the Ink Hero
==================

Playable character for a 2D action platformer in sumi-e ink style: a figure covered in
irezumi tattoos. Movement, five attacks, hurt, death and an ink transformation.

Format: PNG with transparency (RGBA), unless noted. Pivots are in pixels from the
top-left corner of each frame or image. Sprite sheets are a single row of frames
of the listed size, left to right.

Animations
----------
animations/idle: 4 frames, 512x384 px each, 5 fps, loop, pivot (256, 340)
animations/run: 6 frames, 512x384 px each, 10 fps, loop, pivot (256, 340)
animations/jump: 3 frames, 512x384 px each, 4 fps, once, pivot (256, 340)
animations/landing: 2 frames, 512x384 px each, 10 fps, once, pivot (256, 340)
animations/hurt: 2 frames, 512x384 px each, 8 fps, once, pivot (256, 340)
animations/death: 4 frames, 512x384 px each, 7 fps, once, pivot (256, 340)
animations/tiger_claw: 3 frames, 512x384 px each, 12 fps, once, pivot (180, 320)
animations/snake_bite: 3 frames, 512x384 px each, 10 fps, once, pivot (180, 320)
animations/wave_attack: 3 frames, 512x384 px each, 10 fps, once, pivot (180, 320)
animations/koi_dash: 3 frames, 512x384 px each, 12 fps, once, pivot (180, 320)
animations/dragon_special: 4 frames, 512x384 px each, 9 fps, once, pivot (180, 320)
animations/ink_transform: 3 frames, 512x384 px each, 9 fps, once, pivot (180, 320)

Wall moves
----------
animations/wall_grab: 2 frames, 512x384 px each, 6 fps, loop, pivot (256, 340), hanging from a wall edge, facing right
animations/wall_jump: 2 frames, 512x384 px each, 8 fps, once, pivot (256, 340), pushing off the wall
Wall moves come from a separate drawing, scaled to the hero's height; mirror them for the other side.

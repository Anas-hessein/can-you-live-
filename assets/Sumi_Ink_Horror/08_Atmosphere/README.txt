Mist, Wind and Falling Leaves
=============================

A seamless mist strip (tiles horizontally, white with transparency, plus a version on
black for additive or screen blending), four wind brush strokes and six maple leaves.
Mist and wind are white: tint them to suit your background. Leaves are light grey with
darker veins, made to be tinted. All have a centred pivot.

Format: PNG with transparency (RGBA), unless noted. Pivots are in pixels from the
top-left corner of each frame or image. Sprite sheets are a single row of frames
of the listed size, left to right.
